CAPTION / DESCRIPTION
A freezer that deliberately heats its mix? The Taylor C602 uses a heat-treatment cycle, and a missed cooling deadline can leave it soft-locked.

We follow one hypothetical failed cycle from the hopper and freezing barrel to its stage, time and temperature records. Those records can narrow the diagnosis. They don't prove which part failed, and clearing an error is not a substitute for fixing the cause.

This is one cause of unavailability, not an explanation for every McDonald's outage or every machine model. It is an explanation, not repair instructions.
