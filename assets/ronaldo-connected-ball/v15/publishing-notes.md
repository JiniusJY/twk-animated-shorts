## Suggested description
Ronaldo celebrated. But did he touch it?

Inside the connected ball, a suspended inertial measurement unit measures acceleration and rotation. At 500 samples a second, the gap between readings is 2 milliseconds. That is a sampling interval, not a decision every 2 milliseconds.

adidas said Ronaldo's attempted header against Uruguay produced no measurable external force. The goal went to Bruno Fernandes.

A separate use of connected-ball timing is semi-automated offside: timing is combined with camera-tracked player positions, then video officials validate the suggested kick point and offside line.

Visuals are a generic 3D football and conceptual mechanism, signal and position demonstrations. They are not the official Al Rihla design, match footage, recorded match signals or an offside ruling.
