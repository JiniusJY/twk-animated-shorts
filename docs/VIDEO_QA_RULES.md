# Mandatory video safeguards

Owner feedback,23November2026. Apply to every future episode, not only ice cream.

- Preserve accepted assets and story. Make scoped revisions without silently dropping approved subjects.
- Motion must explain: physical cause/effect, changing state, meaningful reveal or data sampled in time. Replace long plain middle holds. Generic scale/zoom is not a substitute.
- Write like a person. Remove repetitive not-X-but-Y contrasts and script scaffolding. Read the full script for these before synthesis.
- Keep only on-screen text that serves the content. Credits and source notes belong in captions/descriptions unless genuinely required on-screen.
- Let the final thought breathe. Check sentence boundaries, word pace, and a deliberate gap before the CTA. Preserve the approved full endcard from its first frame.
- Captions are immutable phrase cues, never a moving per-frame sliding token window. Every spoken token appears once. Repair overlap before grouping, never hide a clause to fix overlap.
- Run scripts/check_caption_cues.py on the source transcript and final cues. Reject duplicated/missing words, backward/overlapping cues, flashing phrases and unreadable length.
- Inspect every caption cue as pixels from the exact exported master. Inspect entrances, exits, section transitions and the CTA gap, not only a contact sheet of six arbitrary frames.
- Listen to the affected audio and inspect motion when capability exists. A transcript/timing check is not acoustic listening. Record the limitation if listening cannot be performed, never silently pass it.
- Verify factual visual semantics: closed lock for lockout, explicit measured units/threshold/deadline where a graph bears the explanation. Mark hypothetical examples through narration/context; do not present invented store records.
- Verify final encoded loudness, peak, black intervals, dimensions, duration and media hash. Upload exact approved bytes and read back the scheduled state.

The renderer, caption cues and final encode are one release candidate. Any change to any of them invalidates the relevant QA evidence. User review is not the first QA layer.

## Phone-app layout and disclosure review, 24 November 2026

- Review each destination separately: Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Full-frame desktop playback is insufficient.
- Keep titles, subtitles, logos and essential action clear of the phone status/cutout band, app header, right action rail and collapsed account/caption/navigation area. Test long and short collapsed captions and the intended cover/feed crop.
- Store per-platform overlay profiles with source URL, retrieval date and whether each margin is an ad-template fallback or an observed organic app measurement. Do not call ad margins an organic guarantee. Device and caption layout vary.
- Export overlay proof images for every title state, every fixed subtitle cue and each key visual transition. Inspect the exact encoded master, fix collisions, then inspect again. Keep full endcard readable. Never solve safety by shrinking all content until unreadable.
- A layout revision keeps narration, music, timing and facts unchanged unless separately requested. Recheck hashes and get review before publishing the changed master. Keep affected unpublished records held; never delete/repost existing live posts as a layout fix without approval.
- No automatic AI-disclosure tick, and no automatic false value. Complete a per-asset, per-platform assessment before release. AI assistance alone is not the test. Flag any specific required exception to the owner before publishing; do not silently override the owner's setting or relabel prior videos.
- No optional public AI/illustration caveats or source dumps by default. Keep the fact-check ledger internal. Preserve required licence credits, and raise a concrete misleading-content risk or mandatory disclosure briefly before publishing. Do not remove an actually required disclosure silently.

## Platform-specific performance checks

- Compare YouTube Shorts only with YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels only with Instagram Reels, and TikTok only with TikTok. Match actual post age and runtime bands where feasible; report the age/freshness gap, not an exact checkpoint when late.
- TikTok: test an immediately understandable first 1-2 seconds and purposeful story progression. Track early retention, average watch time, completion and attributable follows when returned. Do not infer distribution cause from views alone.
- Instagram: inspect first-frame clarity, skip rate, average watch time, reach and shares/saves with their actual definitions. Unsupported completion/follow values remain unavailable, not zero. App-overlay layout is a separate hard QA issue.
- YouTube: separate exposure/shown-in-feed, chose-to-view versus swipe-away, engaged views and retention when available. Low views without exposure/retention data do not establish an opening failure. Avoid quota-heavy repeated deep reads.
- A competitor example is creative evidence, not a matched-age benchmark or proof of why an account grew. A shorter runtime, more movement, a song or a posting hour is not a guaranteed growth fix.
