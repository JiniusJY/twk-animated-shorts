# TWK script editor

Use this writing pass for new or revised TWK shorts after the claim ledger and reference review. It supplements CONTENT_SYSTEM.md, not the owner's decisions or release requirements. Do not modify an accepted cut just to apply a new checklist.

## 1. Hook: clear subject, felt problem, earned question

Draft distinct approaches when useful, not synonyms of one opener. For each, check:
- Does the first spoken beat and picture identify the actual subject?
- Is it clear on one pass, without an empty greeting or teaser?
- Does the viewer understand the relevance to their phone, work, money or daily life?
- Is there a real question or contrast that the body answers?

Use the short-video first-three-second gate for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. Do not apply long-form YouTube's 15-30 second introduction advice to a short. Research remains YouTube and Instagram only; TikTok posting remains unchanged.

Do not manufacture shock, odd numbers, urgency, personal stakes or a false common belief. A straightforward demonstration can work without a contrarian opener. These are writing heuristics, not proven retention laws or a promise of views.

## 2. Story: show what changes and why

Write the factual payoff first, then outline the shortest route to it. Each beat should answer the preceding question, introduce a necessary complication or show its consequence. Try a causal connector such as 'because', 'but' or 'so' to diagnose list-like detail. Do not force those words into every sentence or imply causation the evidence does not establish.

A TWK explainer needs a mechanism or a useful distinction, not an invented protagonist, fake anecdote or manufactured crisis. Match every claim to a visible event. Vary sentence lengths and read the draft as speech to one interested person.

End with the earned answer, then retain the owner's short spoken follow CTA and settled follow/newsletter endcard. A source guide's preference for a looping final line does not remove the owner's CTA.

## 3. Plain explanation: less effort, same truth

Find the densest sentences. Replace unfamiliar jargon or define the necessary term with a concrete example. Prefer active verbs and one new idea at a time. Keep enough variation that speech does not become a series of fragments.

Preserve distinctions, attribution, conditions and uncertainty. A simpler rewrite fails if it changes 'may' to 'will', turns an allegation into a fact or makes a qualitative illustration look measured. Readability grades and words-per-second estimates are rough planning aids. Final length and delivery come from the actual approved voice take, not a formula.

## 4. Specific-writing pass

Remove filler, generic significance, hollow reframing, repeated sentence shapes and claims of authority without a source. State the specific observed behaviour or mechanism. Never invent a number or an event to make prose more concrete.

Keep an explanatory contrast when both sides are accurate and the body supports it. Avoid rigid banned-word substitution that makes the script unnatural. Script, on-screen hook, cover and post copy have different jobs; check each in its real format.

## 5. Voice evidence, not an imitation

Use approved TWK examples to describe the brand's style. Record source links or filenames and distinguish user-written speech, published brand copy and AI-assisted drafts. Do not label assistant-written narration as Jin's personal speaking voice. A personal voice profile needs authentic samples and source-grounded observations. Never send those samples to another service just because a downloaded guide suggests doing so.

A small corpus supports a provisional brand profile, not a confident personal profile or a ranking of 'top-performing' hooks. Performance judgments need comparable, actual-age metrics with availability recorded.

## Audit record

Use docs/SCRIPT_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md in the episode's working folder. Record the exact draft line, the issue, the proposed edit and its truth check. An issue can be 'none found' when that is the result. Do not claim that completing this pass certifies quality or replaces encoded video/audio inspection.
