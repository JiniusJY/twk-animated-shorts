## Claim ledger
1. Ronaldo's head did not touch the ball; the goal was credited to Fernandes.
   Source: adidas statement issued via FIFA to ESPN, 29 Nov 2022; Sky Sports, 29 Nov 2022; The Athletic, 29 Nov 2022.
   Limit: "definitively" stays Adidas's word. Scope the no-force claim to the moment the ball passed Ronaldo's head, per the published graphic. Never narrate it as "no force on the ball" in general.
2. FIFA, not the sensor, awarded the goal: announced for Fernandes in the stadium on the night, confirmed with ball data the next day.
   Source: ESPN, 29 Nov 2022 (espn.in/football/story/_/id/37634372); Sky Sports, 29 Nov 2022.
   Limit: the ball gave evidence; officials made the call. Do not say the sensor overruled anyone.
3. Fernandes quote: "The feeling I had was that Cristiano touched the ball. I was passing the ball to him."
   Source: Reuters, 28 Nov 2022 (reuters.com/lifestyle/sports/my-first-goal-could-have-been-ronaldos-says-portugals-fernandes-2022-11-28); Sky Sports, 29 Nov 2022. Attribution verified 23 Nov 2026.
   Limit: quote only. No invented lines, no fake voice.
4. Match frame: Portugal v Uruguay, 54th minute, 2-0 win, 28 Nov 2022.
   Source: Reuters, ESPN, The Athletic, Indian Express match reporting, 28-30 Nov 2022. Minute verified against multiple reports 23 Nov 2026. Locked.
5. Sensor design: 500Hz IMU in a suspension system at the ball's core, rechargeable by induction, data to video officials within seconds, developed with FIFA and Kinexon.
   Source: Adidas press release, 1 Jul 2022. All from Adidas's own announcement; fine to state as the design.
6. Lukaku equaliser v Slovakia cancelled, 86th minute, Euro 2024, after the ball chip registered Openda's finger brush; broadcast "snicko" heartbeat graphic.
   Source: ESPN VAR review, 17 Jun 2024 (espn.com/football/story/_/id/40371283); L'Equipe, 18 Jun 2024.
   Limit: the chip detected the touch; the handball call was the officials' judgement under UEFA's stricter interpretation. Do not say the ball disallowed the goal.
