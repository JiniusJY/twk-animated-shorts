# TWK editorial short

The default production system is [docs/CONTENT_SYSTEM.md](docs/CONTENT_SYSTEM.md). Read its current constraints and [docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md](docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md) before work. The visual direction is in [references/STYLE_BIBLE.md](references/STYLE_BIBLE.md). Research provenance and limitations are in [docs/QUALITY_RESEARCH.md](docs/QUALITY_RESEARCH.md).

## Production sequence

1. Pick a supportable question and build the source/claim ledger.
2. Write a concise spoken story and a shot-specific storyboard with unique narration anchors.
3. Create original illustrations or rigs that show the mechanism. Do not force unrelated stories into the Boeing pilot scenes.
4. Audition short voice performances. Free Fish model only; no paid or legacy fallback. Review an actual take.
5. Use real aligned words and the shared editorial timing map for picture/actions/SFX.
6. Assemble a short proof, then the whole edit. Review real motion and final sound, not only screenshots.
7. Run automated checks and human release checks. Honor current instructions on workflow dispatch and reruns.
8. Deliver the verified result with material caveats. Do not call a rough cut finished or imply technical QA proves creator-level quality.

The legacy cartoon renderer and bridge assets remain available for an explicit creative choice. Their old camera, grain, character and scene-count rules are not requirements for new episodes. Do not claim internal review scores, specific software brands or generic effects make a video high quality.
